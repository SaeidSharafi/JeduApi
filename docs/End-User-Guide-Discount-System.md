# End User Guide - JeduShop Discount and Order System

## Overview

JeduShop's discount system allows administrators to create flexible promotional campaigns that can automatically apply discounts to products or entire shopping carts. This guide explains how the system works from an end-user perspective.

---

## How Discounts Work

### Types of Discounts

#### 1. Product-Specific Discounts
- Applied directly to individual products
- Automatically calculated and displayed on product pages
- Examples: "25% off all electronics", "Buy photography courses at half price"

#### 2. Cart-Level Discounts
- Applied to your entire cart during checkout
- Can require minimum purchase amounts or specific items
- Examples: "$10 off orders over $100", "Free shipping on orders over $50"

### Discount Priority

When multiple discounts could apply to your order:
1. **Product discounts apply first** - These are already reflected in the prices you see
2. **Cart discounts apply second** - These are calculated when you proceed to checkout
3. **Higher priority discounts apply first** - Some discounts may stop others from applying

---

## Using Coupon Codes

### How to Apply Coupons
1. Add items to your cart
2. Proceed to checkout
3. Enter your coupon code in the "Coupon Code" field
4. Click "Apply" to see the discount applied to your order

### Coupon Rules
- **One coupon per order** - You can only use one coupon code per purchase
- **Usage limits** - Some coupons have limited uses (first 100 customers, etc.)
- **Expiration dates** - Coupons may expire on specific dates
- **Minimum requirements** - Some coupons require minimum purchase amounts

---

## Understanding Your Order

### Order Preview
Before completing your purchase, you'll see:
- **Subtotal** - Original price of all items
- **Discounts Applied** - Total amount saved
- **Grand Total** - Final amount to pay

### Payment Options
- **Full Payment** - Pay the complete amount now
- **Partial Payment** - Make a deposit now, pay remainder later (where available)

### Order Confirmation
After placing your order:
- You'll receive an email confirmation
- Your account will show the order status
- Any course enrollments will be activated after payment

## Common Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Course Sale
1. Browse courses and see sale prices already applied
2. Add courses to cart
3. Proceed to checkout
4. No coupon needed - discount already applied
5. Complete payment

### Scenario 2: Coupon Discount
1. Add items to cart (regular prices shown)
2. Enter coupon code at checkout
3. See discount applied to total
4. Complete payment with reduced amount

### Scenario 3: Minimum Purchase Discount
1. Add items worth less than minimum (no discount)
2. Add more items to reach minimum threshold
3. Discount automatically applies
4. Complete purchase with savings

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why isn't my coupon working?
- **Check spelling** - Coupon codes are case-sensitive
- **Check expiration** - The coupon may have expired
- **Check usage limits** - The coupon may have reached its usage limit
- **Check minimum requirements** - You may need to add more items to qualify

### Can I use multiple coupons?
No, only one coupon code can be applied per order.

### Why do I see different prices?
- **Product discounts** are already shown in the listed price
- **Cart discounts** are calculated at checkout
- **Featured prices** may be temporarily reduced

### What if I need to change my payment?
Contact customer service - they can help with payment modifications or refunds.

### How do enrollments work?
- **Immediate enrollment** - For full payments, you're enrolled immediately
- **Pending enrollment** - For partial payments, enrollment activates after full payment
