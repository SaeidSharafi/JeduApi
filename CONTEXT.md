# JeduShop Domain

JeduShop sells educational content and grants the corresponding access entitlements.

## Catalog and Fulfillment

**Bundle**:
A fixed package of specific Product Delivery Options sold together for one price, without allowing the customer to substitute its components. Its price may equal or be lower than the components' combined prices.
_Avoid_: Package, product group

**Bundle Purchase**:
The customer's acquisition of a Bundle as one commercial unit, granting a separate entitlement for every included Product Delivery Option.
_Avoid_: Individual component purchases

**Bundle Base Value**:
The sum of the undiscounted base prices of a Bundle's component Product Delivery Options at the time of purchase.
_Avoid_: Bundle selling price

**Component Allocation**:
The portion of a Bundle's selling price assigned to one component, representing the amount actually paid for that component.
_Avoid_: Component base price

**Purchase Eligibility**:
A customer is eligible to purchase an offering only when they have not previously acquired its educational content, directly or through a Bundle. Owning any Bundle component makes that Bundle ineligible.
_Avoid_: SKU-only ownership

**Vendor**:
The school department that owns and presents catalog offerings, including its departmental landing page. It is not an external marketplace seller or a financial-settlement boundary.
_Avoid_: Marketplace seller
